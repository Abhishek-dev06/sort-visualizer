Bubble Sort

Repeatedly compares neighboring elements and swaps them when they are in the wrong order.

O(n²)
02

Selection Sort

Finds the smallest remaining element and places it into its final position.

O(n²)
03

Insertion Sort

Builds the sorted section one element at a time by inserting each value into its correct position.

O(n²)
04

Merge Sort

Recursively divides the array, then merges the sorted halves back together.

O(n log n)
05

Quick Sort

Selects a pivot and partitions elements around it, then recursively sorts both sides.

O(n log n)
06

Heap Sort

Builds a max heap and repeatedly moves the largest element to its final position.

O(n log n)
